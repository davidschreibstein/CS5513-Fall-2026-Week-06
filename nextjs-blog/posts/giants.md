The San Francisco Giants are my favorite baseball team. They have been around for well over 100 years, originally playing in New York and moving to San Francisco in the 1950s. They have won 8 total world serieses, including 3 in 5 years from 2010 to 2014. Some all-time great Giants players include:

- Barry Bonds
- Willy Mays
- Tim Lincecum 
- Madison Bumgarner
- Buster Posey
- Willy McCovey
