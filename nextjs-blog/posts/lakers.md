The LA Lakers are my favorite basketball team. They are a very successful franchise having won 17 championships between their time in Minneapolis and Los Angeles. Some of their best all time players include:

- Kobe Bryant
- Magic Johnson
- Shaquille O'Neal
- Kareem Abdul-Jabar 
- Jerry West
- LeBron James
