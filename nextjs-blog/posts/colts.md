The Indianapolis Colts are my favorite football team. They've won two superbowls, one in 1971 and one in 2007. They have been a fairly successful franchise for most of my life however they have struggled for the last 8 years or so, ever since their quarterback Andrew Luck retired. Some All-Time great Colts players are:

- Peyton Manning
- Andrew Luck
- Edgerrin James
- Marvin Harrison
- Johnny Unitas
- Reggie Wayne
